# Great Pyramid / square-pyramid comparison

## Native source architecture

A square pyramid has 5 vertices, 8 unique edges and 5 faces; its four triangular faces have 12 edge incidences.

## Structural extraction

Cardinalities and labels above must be made explicit; cyclicDistance has seven values only for a specified C12 labeling.

## Generative operations

Edge-to-face incidence forgets face labels under projection; repeated incidences do not create new physical edges.
