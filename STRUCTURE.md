# Repository organization: one relational whole

The architecture is invariant relational geometry. Its parts are intelligible
through the complete arrangement, not as independent objects later connected
by a process. Read [INVARIANT.md](INVARIANT.md) for the structural specification.

| Location | Structural role |
|---|---|
| Duodenary/Relational/Whole.lean | The whole and its constitutive fourfold–three-mode differentiation |
| Duodenary/Relational/Relations.lean | Internal relations of succession, opposition, reversal and return |
| Duodenary/Relational/Manifestations.lean | Co-present spatial, type-theoretic and dialogical articulations |
| schemas/relational/ | Declarative relational signature and manifestation family |
| docs/relational/ | Component relationships and structural reading |
| 02_ATLAS/ | Spatial articulation and inspectable finite diagrams |
| 01_CANONICAL/ | Unmodified permanent definition register |
| 03_ENGINE/ and 04_TESTS/ | Preserved engineering manifestations and their local checks |
| verification/ | Preserved mathematical and engineering records |

Lean imports and file references provide access to definitions; they do not
establish temporal, causal or ontological precedence between manifestations.
No new definition assigns a position an identity external to its whole.
No new structural schema is a workflow or translation contract.

## Structural closure, dual registers and admission

- [Closure](Duodenary/Relational/Closure.lean): explicit conjugation and whole-grid integrity.
- [Registers](Duodenary/Relational/Registers.lean): two-way register/JSON state equivalences.
- [Admission](Duodenary/Relational/Admission.lean): constitutive paths and arbitrary compositional closure.
- [Register articulation](docs/relational/DUAL_REGISTERS.md) and [admission relations](docs/relational/ADMISSION_RELATIONS.md).

These are co-present articulations of the primary whole; their file or expression
order does not establish a causal hierarchy between manifestations.
