# The Tobey Machine and Raven — Illustrated Research Roadmap

**Register:** 83 research obligations; **sources:** 157 algorithms, eight Raven plates, 14 sacred seals, 24 original lessons.

## Executive finding
The user attributes the design plates to Raven. Their shared geometry and the seals’ structured command language make it possible to test whether they encode reproducible, source-grounded transformations. The images are evidence to measure, not automatic execution receipts.
