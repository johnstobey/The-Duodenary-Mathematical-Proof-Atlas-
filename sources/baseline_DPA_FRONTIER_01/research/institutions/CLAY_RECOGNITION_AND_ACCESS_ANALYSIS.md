# Clay recognition and access: primary-rule audit

As of 2026-10-31. Primary rule text: https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/millennium_prize_rules_0.pdf (adopted 2018-09-26), and https://www.claymath.org/millennium-problems/rules/ .

| Question | Finding | Controlling provision |
|---|---|---|
| Unaffiliated eligibility | Yes: no affiliation condition; eligible persons exclude specified CMI-connected disqualified persons | §8(d) |
| Direct submissions | Not accepted | §5(e) |
| Qualifying publication | Generally refereed mathematics publication of worldwide repute; named reachable editors, expert refereeing, MathSciNet inclusion | §§6(a),6(e) |
| Relaxation | CMI may waive some §6(e) conditions on expert advice | §6(f) |
| GitHub-only proof | Does not ordinarily satisfy §6(a),(e); discretionary §6(f) path is not guaranteed | §§6(a),6(e),6(f) |
| Lean verification | Supports mathematical checking; does not automatically satisfy qualifying outlet or acceptance | §§4(a–d),7(a) |
| Minimum period | Two years from qualifying publication AND two years rigorous community examination | §§4(b),7(a)(i)(2) |
| General acceptance | Determined by CMI in its sole discretion, with public literature, meetings, awards and other evidence considered | §7(a)(i)(4) |
| Decision authority | CMI has sole authority and its decisions final under rules | §§3(a–c),7(a)(ii),8(a) |
| Confidentiality | Deliberations private; records normally unavailable without board approvals until 50 years; no explanation owed | §3(d) |
| Prize attribution | Prior insights may be credited or shared; CMI may decline award when uncertain | §§8(b–c) |
| Mathematical proof validity | Logically distinct from prize decision; rules define prize process, not a general validity test | §§4,6,7 |

The rules create recognized practical publication barriers but do not establish a ban on unaffiliated mathematicians, proof invalidity, or institutional misconduct. Publicly timestamped git commits, archived releases, tagged sources and independently reproducible Lean projects are useful priority records, not substitutes for Clay §6 qualifying publication.
