# Newly queued structural bridge

Candidate: for any homomorphism from the cyclic calendar group Z/260Z to Z/12Z, the image has cardinality dividing four; consequently no surjective homomorphism exists. This is a standard finite-group consequence, not a novelty claim. The finite obstruction was checked; a general Lean proof is queued as T-AZTEC-MAP. Cultural applicability requires independently sourced calendrical operations.
Failed bridge: an accelerated Collatz step does not descend to the naive mod-12 quotient: 1 and 13 share a residue, but (3n+1)/2 gives residues 2 and 8. This blocks that particular quotient construction, not Collatz itself.
