# Processor comparison

The Atlas constructs four unit slopes times twelve translations. This yields 48 distinct affine permutations with composition (a,b)(c,d)=(ac,ad+b). Only slopes ±1 preserve the named undirected cyclic distances: multiplication by 5 sends distance 1 to distance 5. The 24 dihedral maps are a proper subset of the 48 affine maps.
An unrelated source's 48 operations are not identified by cardinality. Exact source operations, types, composition and labeled inverse tables are required; equivalence remains unresolved. No private implementation was ported.
The 144-state product is a set bijection with Fin 144, while (Z/12Z)² and Z/144Z have different additive exponents. Encoding alone is not a group isomorphism.
