# Encoding and engineering boundaries

Four binary bits distinguish twelve values with four unused codes. A 12-channel physical minimum does not follow from a twelve-state mathematical model. Square-pyramid face-edge incidences (12) differ from unique edges (8). Source 44% similarity lacks its original denominator/table and remains unreproduced.
Corvus and Polyphemus remain source-reported engineering/rhetorical candidates. No automatic security, temporal continuity, consciousness or immunity property is established by these finite checks. Such claims need an explicit threat model, operational definitions and adversarial tests.
